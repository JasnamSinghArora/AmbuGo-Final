Figure. Joint ambulance routing and selective signal priority with a causal progress guard. Received counts and observed executed progress on regular road links determine each ambulance's mode. Selective mode evaluates a finite route–action library using a predicted time band and an approximate ordinary-traffic burden; an irreversible progress-triggered switch invokes aggressive joint routing and preemption. Both modes share request arbitration, service-age protection and legal signal clearances. The 8 s green label denotes a minimum, while amber, all-red and the reserved pedestrian opportunity last 3, 2 and 25 s. The separate paired-S0 ledger (executable policy P0) evaluates signed restricted residence change ΔJ, positive spatial exposure A and positive individual residence burden D⁺ from logged trajectories; those realized evaluation quantities are never controller inputs. No physical pedestrians or field calibration are implied.
