AmbuGo independent mesoscopic verification reference
25 October 2026

Files and scope
meso.py contains an independent numerical verification reference and secondary network scaffold. meso_checks.py executes the checks. meso_verification.json contains the actual run outputs. These files do not reproduce the teammate's unavailable v8 implementation and do not validate a measured urban corridor. They also do not establish agreement between the main SUMO controller and this mesoscopic scaffold; that would require an explicit harmonized-input experiment. The network performance values in the verification JSON are stress-test diagnostics, not main comparative evidence.

Actual numerical evidence
All 24 checks pass. The full-network component audited 33,600 ticks in six nominal/preemption runs spanning demand multipliers 1, 4 and 12, plus one repeated nominal run. Conservation, unique vehicle identity, PCU bookkeeping, physical packing and the complete-cohort area identity are asserted on every tick. The heaviest case retains an external queue of 1,424 vehicles; desired departures are not dropped or counted as completed. The repeated seed is bit-reproducible, and policy pairs have identical generated cohorts. These are software verification claims within this model.

The fluid pulse calculation was checked at X = .50, .70, .85 and .90, c = .5 vehicle/s and Delta = 10 s. Exact areas are 25, 58.3333333333, 141.6666666667 and 225 vehicle-seconds. Independent piecewise-linear ODE integration differs by at most 2.9e-12 vehicle-seconds. Deterministic fine-packet calculations with masses .25, .10, .025 and .005 show decreasing absolute errors in every setting. At packet mass .005, relative area errors are .300%, .247%, .222% and .215%. A fine-packet mass is a numerical discretization, not a realistic fractional road vehicle.

Correct analytic statement and proof
Let lambda be a constant fluid arrival rate, c > lambda a constant service capacity, and Delta > 0 a service blackout. Start both treatment and baseline empty at t = 0. The baseline has constant capacity c, so its queue is zero. The treatment has no service on [0,Delta), then capacity c. Its queue is lambda*t for 0 <= t <= Delta and [lambda*Delta - (c-lambda)*(t-Delta)]_+ thereafter. Thus it empties at R = Delta + lambda*Delta/(c-lambda) = c*Delta/(c-lambda). Its additional residence area is the area of a triangle of height lambda*Delta and base R: A = lambda*c*Delta^2/[2(c-lambda)]. With X = lambda/c this is A = c*Delta^2*X/[2(1-X)]. Consequently A*(1-X)/X = c*Delta^2/2 exactly in this toy model. A pure k/(1-X) fit describes only the asymptotic denominator; the numerator X cannot be suppressed when claiming an exact load law over a broad range. Nothing in this proof establishes a universal signalized-network delay law, a gridlock threshold at X=.9, or the same coefficient under cyclic service, stochastic demand, finite storage or interacting corridors.

Conservative packet model
Each link has one FIFO queue per declared lane and a deterministic free-flow readiness clock. Receiving storage must satisfy two independent constraints: PCU occupancy below 0.16 PCU per usable metre, and actual sum of vehicle length plus gap below usable lane metres. These are synthetic model assumptions. Vehicles reserve receiving space from the old state, so a space liberated on the same tick is conservatively unavailable until the next tick. A vehicle can cross at most one link boundary on a tick. Each lane accumulates service credit only while its head is ready and its approach is green after a two-second start-up delay. Accepted discharge retains the fractional remaining credit; blocked discharge caps credit at the current head's PCU. Empty and red service lose credit. This avoids inventing fractional bus departures and avoids discarding all fractional credit after each accepted vehicle.

Every generated packet exists in exactly one of the external source queues, a link lane, or the completion list. Reservations prevent concurrent upstream entries from overfilling one receiving lane. Removing accepted senders and then adding reserved arrivals preserves conservation and storage by construction. Runtime assertions challenge these invariants under substantial oversaturation.

The signal reference has exclusive approach greens, then full 3 s amber and 2 s all-red, plus a 25 s noninterruptible pedestrian occupancy reservation. Preemption can shorten the current green only after its 8 s minimum and can select an approach only after clearance. No discharge is allowed on amber in this independent reference. The pedestrian block is an occupancy reservation, not a simulated pedestrian stream or proof of pedestrian safety. The scaffold supplies no hard 180 s individual wait guarantee.

Restricted cohort accounting
For every vehicle with desired release r_v < H and completion d_v, define r_v <= d_v, with min(H,d_v)=H for unfinished trips. Including external-source waits, the residence integral is J_H = integral_0^H N(t)dt = sum_v [min(H,d_v)-r_v]_+. The equality follows by interchanging the finite sum and integral of each vehicle's presence indicator. This is a sample-path area identity, and does not require Little's stationary averaging theorem. The numerical reference uses grid-rounded desired releases and completion times; the identity holds exactly to floating arithmetic in all audited runs. Comparing policy pairs requires the same desired cohort. A difference in this residence integral is a difference in conventional route-relative delay only if the total free-flow reference of that cohort is equal across compared controls. Censored-area results are restricted-horizon quantities and are not automatically lower bounds on eventual signed extra delay.

Secondary network scaffold
The scaffold is callable as simulate_network(network_dir, seed=0, demand_scale=1, horizon_s=2400, missions_per_hour=2, policy='nominal', dt=.25). The alternative policy is 'preempt'. It retains the 59-link v7 geometry. It generates four near-shortest simple routes within 1.25 times free-flow minimum, with logit choice. Ordinary routes stay fixed after release. A stationary lognormal AR1 shock is shared across origins, and independent SHA256-namespaced streams generate origins, classes, routes and missions. Each origin's base rate is .025 vehicle/s times demand_scale. There is no conversion from demand_scale to critical degree of saturation X. Fixed 24 s approach greens are used; this is not a Webster-optimized or demand-calibrated main controller. Repeated ambulances are generated through an exponential mission process but any numerical performance depends on this scaffold's limited control rules.

Limits to retain in a paper
No physical car-following, lane changing, lateral yielding, connector geometry, sublane packing, noisy sensor assimilation, turning-specific lanes or calibrated class PCU equivalences are represented. FIFO point queues do not supply a backward-moving spatial shock model. Neither this reference nor passing analytic checks establishes field accuracy or the safety of deployed priority. The proper role is to verify accounting, storage, conservative clearance and the exact analytic toy statement, while the main traffic simulator supplies separately measured synthetic performance and later corridor measurements supply empirical calibration.
