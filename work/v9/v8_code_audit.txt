Independent audit of the supplied AmbuGo v8 implementation
25 October 2026

Scope
Reviewed ambugo/sim.py, policies.py, network.py, design.py, scenario.py, scripts/run_experiments.py and tests/run_tests.py from work/v9/teammate_v8/ambugo_v8. The original source package was not modified. Executed focused adversarial regression cases in work/v9/v8_regression_checks.py. These cases supplement, rather than replace, the teammate's existing verification suite.

Release finding
The supplied implementation supports real reproducible synthetic experiments, but the original report's unqualified finite-storage and shared-capacity claims are false for ambulances. The current test suite explicitly permits those exceptions and therefore cannot establish the stronger invariant stated in its documentation. A narrowly corrected clone is available at work/v9/v8_corrected. Its changes must be reported as mechanical sensitivity, with all controller/design assumptions otherwise held fixed.

Executed counterexamples against untouched v8
1. Ambulance storage bypass. Simulator.has_space returns True for vid == ambulance irrespective of receiving occupancy. At desired dispatch the ambulance also calls enter directly, 20 m inside the source link, without admission flow or storage checks. A test link filled to 2.0 PCU becomes 3.5 PCU when the 1.5-PCU ambulance is dispatched. Runtime audit reports no violations because _audit explicitly allows storage + ambulance PCU whenever the ambulance is present. This can change route availability and priority effectiveness precisely when downstream storage binds.
2. Ambulance service bypass. In stop-line discharge, both the insufficient-credit test and credit subtraction exclude the ambulance. In an executable case with exactly zero approach discharge capacity, the ambulance crosses at t=48 s and arrives at t=98 s; audit reports no violations. At positive capacity .5 PCU/s, its 1.5-PCU traversal leaves 1.0 PCU service credit unconsumed. This creates capacity rather than merely changing legal signal order.
3. Source insertion privilege. Direct insertion bypasses the external ordinary-traffic FIFO and the origin approach's PCU admission budget. Moving the ambulance 20 m downstream also changes its free-flow travel clock. Even if treated as an intentional ambulance access privilege, this must be declared and challenged; it contradicts a no-speed/no-extra-capacity interpretation.
4. Unfinished stop-line waits omitted. The wait metrics update only when an ordinary vehicle discharges. In a controlled blocked-link case a vehicle has already waited 348 s at the final tick, but reported max_wait=0 and n_wait_over=0. Runtime audit reports no violations. Those outputs measure discharged wait episodes, not all experienced waits. Any claim to bound vehicle protection risk must include unfinished wait episodes and explicit censoring.
All counterexamples and original audit outputs are in v8_regression_findings.json.

Minimal corrected sensitivity
The cloned sim.py changes only ambulance admission, service and associated bookkeeping:
- Desired ambulance dispatch joins the same origin source FIFO as ordinary desired releases; it is enqueued once.
- Source admission obeys shared PCU credit and strict receiving storage, and starts at position zero.
- Stop-line discharge requires and debits ambulance PCU exactly as for every other vehicle.
- has_space and _audit allow no ambulance-specific storage overflow.
- Ordinary conservation excludes a queued ambulance from external ordinary counts.
- Ambulance travel remains measured from desired dispatch; source waiting therefore contributes. Results add amb_insertion and amb_source_wait.
No policies, signal plans, demand law, yielding law, sensor law, ordinary routing or exit-link model were changed. The clone is not retuned. Eighteen focused and stress checks pass, including a zero-capacity incident starting after the ambulance is already admitted, a full receiving link causing 43 s source wait, six full-network high-load/incident/yielding runs, and strict ordinary conservation. Evidence: v8_corrected/results/mechanical_verification.json. These checks establish the repaired mechanics within this model; they do not imply city accuracy.

Accurate or useful features retained
Ordinary fractional PCU discharge credits are generally preserved after accepted service; the ambulance exemption is the major exception. Ordinary external source waiting is counted in the released-minus-completed population. Exit transport times are kept in residence accounting even after vehicles leave the explicit deque. Stage advancement preserves the specified green/amber/all-red/pedestrian order, and skip overrides are applied at all-red boundaries. The pedestrian occupancy block is not shortened. The baseline wait guard is shared by all policies, avoiding a clear adaptive-control advantage for priority arms. Ordinary demand, incidents and sensor streams are separated by namespaces. Training and evaluation replication ranges are separated in the script. The public-versus-hidden export test is useful for exported fixture leakage, although it does not prove every controller call respects all information boundaries.

Remaining model limits and interpretation repairs
- Multi-lane queues are not lane-resolved. A single deque exposes its first `lanes` vehicles and allows limited overtaking if one of those destinations is blocked. There is no lane ID or per-lane FIFO, lane-changing process or turning-pocket geometry. This is a pooled-head mesoscopic approximation and must not be described as a physically resolved multi-lane model.
- PCU storage is an aggregate design closure. It does not check sum of vehicle lengths/gaps or lateral packing. Buses and motorcycles are weighted in aggregate but physical spacing and turning geometry remain absent.
- Exit links have unbounded free-flow transport by design, bypassing downstream source/queue limitations. That is a declared boundary condition, not a numerical storage check. Boundary expansion and endpoint placement can affect conclusions.
- Discharge credits are capped at one tick's approach service while no eligible receiving head is found. Some one-tick credit can persist while the head is not yet ready. These discretization conventions remain in the narrow correction and deserve step-size sensitivity rather than being described as exact physical discharge.
- Yielding reorders queued vehicles without testing lateral receiving space. A single sequential yield RNG stream is consumed in policy-dependent events; the same driver's yielding draw is not keyed to vehicle ID across controls. Shared seeds remain a coupling, but do not establish an identical vehicle-level yielding realization. A vehicle/mission-keyed behavioral field would strengthen common-random-number alignment.
- `max_red` measures demanded inter-green time and `max_ped_wait` measures gaps between walk starts. Neither is the distribution of individual pedestrian or vehicle delays. No pedestrian arrival process or crossing trajectories are simulated.
- Observed baseline wait violations establish failure of this controller/model setting, not infeasibility of every admissible signal policy at that nominal X. With unbounded Poisson bursts, a deterministic bound for every vehicle is impossible at every positive load without additional admission or support assumptions. Relative-risk or probabilistic protection statements are more defensible.
- A perfect-state run of a fixed heuristic is a policy-specific information ablation. It is not an upper bound on every estimator's or redesigned controller's possible gain. The standard value-of-information inequality requires optimization over a common policy class and information ordering, not this one plug-in comparison.
- A one-second timing nudge is a real intervention. Its between-scenario distribution quantifies local sensitivity and heterogeneity, not a universal noise floor, nor mathematical chaos. The required replication count depends on the endpoint, paired variance, precision target and effect margin.
- The ordinary occupancy-area identity is a finite-sample residence accounting identity, not a direct invocation of stationary Little's law. Including external waiting is a strength. A residence difference equals a conventional delay difference only under a common fixed free-flow reference, as with the ordinary routes here.
- A finite-horizon signed extra-residence estimate is not automatically a lower bound on eventual cost. Future signed differences can be negative. A cumulative positive-part burden is monotone, while the signed integral is not. Failure to recover by H should be reported as censored follow-up.
- Confidence intervals containing zero do not establish equivalence, no cost, or equal cost between phase-skipping and truncation. Define a meaningful margin and use an equivalence/noninferiority analysis if that claim is central.
- The observed X axis is an ex ante PCU flow-to-plan-capacity surrogate. Guard-induced plan departures, incidents, lane pooling, blocked receiving space and transient demand change realized service. No universal traffic-density or gridlock threshold follows from it.
- The exact isolated constant-arrival pulse calculation in meso_notes.txt has area c*Delta^2*X/[2(1-X)] under explicitly empty baseline and constant service. A fitted k/(1-X) network curve is empirical and should be compared with alternative load curves; T5's tolerance check is not a theorem validating the network law.

Recommendations for the first paper
Report the supplied v8 experiment as a reproducibility baseline only after raw-row checks. Present paired mechanical-sensitivity results from the corrected clone as a central robustness test. Implement the new joint/selective controller and interacting missions in a detailed simulator with a documented mapping, rather than calling the mesoscopic clone calibrated. Keep microscopic outcomes and mesoscopic outcomes in separate tables unless their inputs and modeled mechanisms are explicitly harmonized. Record all unfinished missions, source delay, terminal backlog and positive spatial burden. Analyze multiple missions at episode/seed level rather than treating correlated missions as independent trials. Retain real-corridor calibration as a genuine remaining empirical task.
